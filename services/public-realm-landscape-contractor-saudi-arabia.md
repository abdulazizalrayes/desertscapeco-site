## Common client questions.

### What is public realm landscaping?

Public realm landscaping covers outdoor spaces used by the public, including parks, plazas, streetscapes, civic landscapes, open spaces, pedestrian areas, and community destinations.

### Can DesertScape support parks and streetscapes?

Yes. DesertScape can support parks, plazas, streetscapes, planting, irrigation, hardscape, furniture, lighting, play areas, and handover requirements.

### Does public realm work need maintenance planning?

Yes. Public landscapes need practical maintenance access, irrigation tuning, plant health routines, replacement planning, safety review, and asset condition reporting.
