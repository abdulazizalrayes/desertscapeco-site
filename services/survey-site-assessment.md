# Survey and Site Assessment

Site review, measurements, access study, constraints mapping, utilities awareness, soil observations, existing asset inventory, and early delivery notes.

## Scope.

- Site visit and visual review
- Existing tree, palm, softscape, hardscape, and irrigation review
- Access, staging, logistics, and buildability notes
- Risk notes for drainage, soil, utilities, and phasing

Each service can be provided as a standalone scope or connected to other DesertScape services depending on the project stage.
