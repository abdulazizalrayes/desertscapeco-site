## Common client questions.

### Where should landscape automation equipment be located?

Landscape automation equipment is usually planned in technical or back-of-house areas with controlled access, service clearance, cabling routes, ventilation, protection, and maintenance visibility.

### Can automation improve irrigation performance?

Yes. Controllers, sensors, weather data, pump and valve automation, alerts, and monitoring can improve response, water-use review, and maintenance planning.

### Can landscape IoT connect to BMS or operations teams?

Yes. Landscape automation can be coordinated with BMS platforms, IoT monitoring, facilities teams, and maintenance teams depending on the project requirements.
