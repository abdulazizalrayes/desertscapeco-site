# About DesertScape Co

DesertScape Co is a Riyadh based landscape company built for complete outdoor project delivery.

![Diriyah style public realm landscape](https://www.desertscapeco.com/assets/visual-about-diriyah-public-realm.jpg)

## We plant life into spaces that need lasting performance.

The company focuses on the full landscape lifecycle. That includes site review, design support, procurement, construction, testing, handover, operation, and maintenance.

DesertScape is being built for clients who value clarity, project discipline, climate aware design, and dependable execution.

### Vision

To become a trusted landscape partner for high quality outdoor environments across Saudi Arabia.

### Mission

To deliver landscape solutions that are practical, elegant, maintainable, and aligned with Saudi project expectations.

### Operating Model

A coordinated delivery model across design, supply, build, maintenance, vendor networks, and quality documentation.

## Team.

DesertScape Co is built around an in-house multidisciplinary team with strong local Saudi experience and international project exposure. Our people come from engineering, technical, landscape, construction, operations, and QHSE backgrounds, with hands-on experience in mega-project delivery, fast-track programs, complex site coordination, and high-standard project execution. The team is supported by a capable technical office that manages drawings, specifications, quantities, planning, documentation, quality controls, and coordination from early project stages through handover and maintenance.

![DesertScape team reviewing private resort landscape construction](https://www.desertscapeco.com/assets/visual-team-private-resort-construction.jpg)

![DesertScape in-house equipment yard with trucks, pickups, loaders, generators, and tower lights](https://www.desertscapeco.com/assets/visual-assets-equipment-yard.jpg)

## Asset Capabilities.

DesertScape Co operates with in-house machinery, equipment, trucks, vehicles, logistics resources, tools, and site support assets needed for reliable landscape delivery. Our assets are selected to be new or relatively new, maintained to project standards, and third-party certified where required, allowing the company to mobilize efficiently, control quality, and support projects with dependable field capability.
