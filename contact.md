## Frequently asked questions.

### What does DesertScape Co do?

DesertScape Co provides landscape survey, design, supply, construction, operation, and maintenance services for projects in Saudi Arabia.

### Does DesertScape provide landscape supply?

Yes. Supply and procurement can cover plants, palms, trees, turf, planters, hardscape materials, irrigation systems, lighting, outdoor furniture, playground items, water feature components, and specialist landscape products.

### Which landscape elements are covered?

Softscape, hardscape, irrigation, water features, civil works, MEP works, infrastructure, lighting, outdoor furniture, public realm works, playgrounds, nursery, tree removal, tree relocation, tree supply, tree production, indoor plants, planters, parks, and open spaces.

### Which sectors does DesertScape serve?

Hospitality, residential, compounds, governmental projects, entertainment, sports venues, hospitals, parks, commercial developments, mixed use developments, mega projects, and high end private clients.

### Does DesertScape cover all Saudi Arabia?

DesertScape is based in Riyadh and is structured to support projects across Saudi Arabia through project based mobilization, supply, vendor coordination, and maintenance planning.

### Can DesertScape maintain a project after handover?

Yes. Operation and maintenance can be planned as a standalone service or as part of a design, supply, and construction package to protect the landscape after opening.

### Can vendors register with DesertScape?

Yes. Suppliers, subcontractors, nurseries, manufacturers, and specialist vendors can send a company profile, CR, VAT, catalogs, certificates, references, and contact details to the company email.
