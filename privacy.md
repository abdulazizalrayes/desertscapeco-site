# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-06-09.

### Information Received

We may receive information you send by email or forms, including name, company, contact details, project information, vendor documents, or CVs.

### Use

Information is used to respond to inquiries, assess opportunities, manage vendors, review career interest, and improve business communications.

### Analytics and Consent

Where consent is required, optional analytics and advertising measurement storage remain off until you choose otherwise. Advertising personalization and user-provided advertising data remain disabled.

### Cookie Choices

You can accept, reject, or change optional measurement choices at any time using Cookie settings in the website footer.

### Sharing

Information may be shared with DesertScape team members, service providers, or advisers as needed for business purposes.

### Contact

Privacy questions may be sent to projects@desertscapeco.com.
